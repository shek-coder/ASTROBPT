# Realtime Technology Simulation

The four technology cards are wired directly to `RealtimeTechnologySimulation.jsx`.

Click any technology card to open its dedicated live simulation:
- MFC: microbial oxidation, Geobacter/anode concept, electron transfer
- DUFC: urea molecules, catalytic anode, electrochemical oxidation
- Anaerobic Digestion + Fuel Cell: solid waste, anaerobic digestion, biogas, fuel cell
- Thermochemical Processing: dried waste, high-temperature reactor, syngas, generator

Controls are deterministic educational model inputs; no random values are used. Outputs are labeled estimated/simulated.
